Why a thought assistant

Answers are cheap now. Understanding is not.

A fluent reply can hide weak evidence, convenient assumptions, and real disagreement. It ends the inquiry at exactly the point where a researcher would begin.

TruthLM does the opposite. It slows a question down, shows how it could be answered, and gives the thinking back to you.

How an investigation thinks

Seven questions every investigation must answer

Our method is a sequence of questions, not a prompt. Every published investigation answers them in the open, and you can ask the same questions of any claim you meet.

  1. 01

    What exactly is being asked?

    Pin down the population, outcome, time frame, and terms, so the question can be wrong in a specific way.

  2. 02

    What would count as good evidence?

    Set the standard before looking, so the evidence cannot be chosen to fit a conclusion.

  3. 03

    What does the research literature actually show?

    Retrieve and read legally sourced papers, and separate what studies measured from what they claimed.

  4. 04

    Do the numbers hold up?

    Recompute equations and statistics with deterministic tools instead of trusting quoted figures.

  5. 05

    Where do informed people disagree, and why?

    Independent domain experts challenge each other. Disagreement is recorded, not averaged away.

  6. 06

    What would change the conclusion?

    Name the assumptions, boundary cases, and missing evidence the answer depends on.

  7. 07

    What should be investigated next?

    Every investigation ends with the questions it could not settle. That is where your thinking starts.

From question to investigation

A small research community, not a social network.

There are no feeds, followers, likes, or comment threads. Members bring questions that deserve research, and everyone else reads what those questions become.

Domains we cover now: {{ domain_labels.values() | join(' · ') }}

  1. 01

    Members ask

    Invited students and researchers submit questions that cannot be settled by a lookup or a calculator.

  2. 02

    A person reviews every question

    Nothing is shown to other members before review. Inappropriate questions, and questions with a simple answer, are declined with a reason.

  3. 03

    Members prioritize within each domain

    Members upvote the questions they most want investigated. Upvotes decide what we look at, not what is true.

  4. 04

    We investigate and publish weekly

    Each week we take the top question in each domain, run the full investigation, and publish it here for anyone to read and share.
