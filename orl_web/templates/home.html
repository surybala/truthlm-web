Questions before answers

AI can give you an answer in seconds. TruthLM is for questions worth slowing down for. We investigate the evidence, check the numbers, and publish the work so you can decide what to make of it.

See how the question was scoped, what the evidence shows, which numbers were checked, where experts disagree, and what is still unknown.

Read the articles Subscribe to new research

Answers are cheap now. Understanding is not.

A fluent reply can hide weak evidence, convenient assumptions, and real disagreement. It ends the inquiry at exactly the point where a researcher would begin.

TruthLM does the opposite. It slows a question down, shows how it could be answered, and gives the thinking back to you.

How TruthLM turns a question into grounded research

A research director coordinates independent domain experts, relevant literature, and an evidence-grounded language model. Drafts go through repeated review until the important issues are resolved.
